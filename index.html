Got Questions?

Is trading risky?

Yes. Forex, CFDs and other leveraged products can involve substantial risk. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Do I need experience?

No experience is required to start learning. Begin with the platform and demo account basics.

Do I need to pay Lutan?

This recreation includes free educational content. Check the VIP page for the site's membership presentation.

What if I lose money?

Losses are possible. Use appropriate risk controls, understand leverage and practice on a demo account first.

How do I receive signals?

Signals, if offered, should be treated as educational information rather than a guarantee of profit. Always verify a setup and its risks yourself.
